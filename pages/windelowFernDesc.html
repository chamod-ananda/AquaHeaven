Windelow Fern


Windeløv Fern is a hardy, slow-growing aquatic plant with finely branched, lacy leaf tips that create a
decorative, bushy appearance. It typically grows up to 15-25 cm tall, making it ideal for midground placement
in an aquarium. Like other Java Fern varieties, it thrives when attached to rocks or driftwood, as burying
its rhizome in the substrate can cause it to rot. It prefers low to moderate lighting, does not require CO₂
supplementation, and is highly adaptable to various water conditions. Windeløv Fern propagates through
rhizome division or by producing small plantlets on its leaves. Its unique leaf structure makes it a popular
choice for aquascaping, adding texture and visual interest while providing shelter for fish and shrimp.

Plant Info
Type Rhizome Plant
Origin Cultivar (derived from Java Fern)
Growth Rate Slow to Moderate
Height 15 - 25 cm (6 - 10 inches)
Light Demand Low to Medium
CO2 Low (does not require injection)
