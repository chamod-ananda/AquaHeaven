Barclaya


Barclaya longifolia, often simply referred to as Barclaya, is a unique and captivating aquatic plant that belongs to the water lily family. Unlike most water lilies, Barclaya longifolia grows entirely submerged, with long, narrow leaves that can range in color from green to a striking reddish-purple. Native to Southeast Asia, this plant adds an elegant and exotic touch to aquariums. It is known for its ability to flower completely underwater, producing small, delicate blooms. Barclaya longifolia develops a bulb-like structure as it matures, which stores nutrients and helps the plant withstand challenging conditions. While it can be a bit more demanding than some other aquarium plants, requiring soft water and moderate to high lighting, it rewards aquarists with its graceful appearance and the potential for underwater blooms.

Plant Info
Type Bulb Plant
Origin Southeast Asia
Growth Rate Moderate to Fast
Height 20 - 50 cm (8 - 20 inches)
Light Demand Medium to High
CO2 Medium (benefits from CO₂ injection for better growth)
