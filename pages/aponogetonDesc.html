Aponogeton


Aponogeton is a diverse genus of aquatic plants known for its graceful, flowing leaves and unique, often
fragrant flowers. Depending on the species, Aponogeton plants can range from delicate, narrow leaves to
broad, ruffled foliage, adding elegance to any aquarium. Growing between 20-50 cm in height, they are
best suited as midground or background plants. Thriving in moderate to high lighting with nutrient-rich
substrates, they benefit from CO₂ supplementation but can adapt to low-tech setups. Many species undergo
a dormancy period, requiring a rest phase before regrowth. Their striking appearance and easy maintenance
make them a favorite for aquascapers looking to add texture and movement to their planted tanks.

Plant Info
Type Rhizome Plant
Origin Africa, Asia, Madagascar
Growth Rate Moderate to Fast
Height 30 - 60 cm (12 - 24 inches)
