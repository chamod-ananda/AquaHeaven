Coral Moss


Coral Moss (Riccardia chamedryfolia) is a delicate, slow-growing liverwort known for its intricate, coral-like branching structure. Its dense, compact growth and deep green coloration make it a popular choice for aquascaping, especially in nano tanks and nature-style setups. Thriving under moderate to high lighting with CO₂ supplementation, Coral Moss attaches well to hardscape elements like driftwood and rocks, creating a natural, aged appearance. It propagates by fragmentation, allowing easy attachment and spreading over surfaces. Ideal for shrimp tanks, Coral Moss provides shelter and biofilm for grazing, enhancing both the aesthetic and ecological balance of an aquarium.

Plant Info
Type Liverwort (Moss)
Origin Southeast Asia
Growth Rate Slow
Height 1 - 3 cm (0.4 - 1.2 inches)
Light Demand Medium to High
CO2 Medium to High (CO₂ injection recommended for best growth)
