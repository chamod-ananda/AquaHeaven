Alternanthera Reineckii


Alternanthera Reineckii is a striking, colorful stem plant known for its vibrant red to pinkish hues,
which can intensify under high lighting and with proper CO₂ supplementation. With its elongated,
lance-shaped leaves, this plant creates a bold visual contrast in aquariums, making it an ideal
choice for the midground or background. Growing up to 30-50 cm in height, it thrives in
nutrient-rich substrates and benefits from regular pruning to encourage bushier growth and
maintain its shape. Alternanthera Reineckii does best in moderate to high lighting conditions
but can adapt to lower light levels, though its color may fade. Its stunning coloration and
unique texture make it a popular choice for aquascapers looking to add vibrancy and depth to
their planted tanks.

Plant Info
Type Stem Plant
Origin South America
Growth Rate Moderate to Fast
Height 20 - 50 cm (8 - 20 inches)
Light Demand Medium to High
CO2 Medium to High (CO₂ injection improves growth and color)
