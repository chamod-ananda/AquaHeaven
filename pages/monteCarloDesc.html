Monte Carlo


Micranthemum 'Monte Carlo' is a low-growing carpeting plant that forms a dense, vibrant green mat
in freshwater aquariums. With small, round leaves and creeping stems, it spreads quickly across the
substrate, creating a lush, natural look. Native to Argentina, Monte Carlo is an excellent alternative
to Hemianthus callitrichoides (HC Cuba) due to its easier care and adaptability. It thrives in nutrient-rich
substrates with moderate to high lighting and benefits from CO₂ supplementation for faster growth. Regular
trimming helps maintain its compact form. When attached to rocks or driftwood, it can also create unique
aquascaping effects, adding depth and texture to the aquarium.

Plant Info
Type Carpet Plant
Origin Argentina
Growth Rate Moderate to Fast
Height 3 - 5 cm (1 - 2 inches)
Light Demand Medium to High
CO2 Medium (15-25 mg/L) recommended for compact growth
