Bacopa


Bacopa, a genus of hardy, fast-growing stem plants, is well-known for its small, oval-shaped green leaves that grow in pairs along sturdy stems. Popular species like Bacopa monnieri (Moneywort) and Bacopa caroliniana can reach heights of 30-50 cm, making them ideal for midground or background placement in aquariums. Bacopa thrives in moderate to high lighting, with stronger light enhancing its compact growth and, in some species, bringing out reddish hues. While CO₂ supplementation boosts its growth, it can also flourish in low-tech setups. It propagates easily through stem cuttings, making it a great choice for aquascaping. Bacopa’s upright structure and vibrant greenery add contrast to planted tanks while providing shelter for aquatic life.

Plant Info
Type Stem Plant
Origin Worldwide (Tropical & Subtropical)
Growth Rate Moderate to Fast
Height 20 - 50 cm (8 - 20 inches)
Light Demand Medium to High
CO2 Low to Medium (benefits from CO₂ injection but not required)
