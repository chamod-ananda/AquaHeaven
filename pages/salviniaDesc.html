Salvinia


Salvinia is a fast-growing, free-floating aquatic fern known for its small, feathery, and bright green leaves. The plant forms dense mats on the water’s surface, where its leaves have a unique texture, with the upper surface being slightly hairy, helping it to stay afloat. Salvinia's roots hang beneath the water, providing shelter and filtering excess nutrients, thus helping control algae growth. It thrives in moderate to high lighting and prefers calm waters, with warmer temperatures promoting faster growth. Salvinia spreads quickly through runners and can easily cover the surface, requiring regular thinning to prevent overcrowding. While beneficial for water quality, it can block light for submerged plants if left unchecked.

Plant Info
Type Floating Plant
Origin South America, Southeast Asia
Growth Rate Fast
Height 3 - 5 cm (1 - 2 inches), spreads over surface
Light Demand Medium to High
CO2 Low (absorbs CO₂ from air, does not require injection)
