Weeping Moss


Vesicularia ferriei, commonly known as Weeping Moss, is a beautiful, cascading aquatic moss known for its downward-hanging, drooping fronds that create a lush, weeping effect. Its deep green, soft-textured branches attach well to driftwood, rocks, or mesh, making it ideal for creating natural-looking aquascapes, moss walls, or tree-like structures. Weeping Moss thrives in moderate to high lighting and benefits from CO₂ supplementation for faster growth, though it can also survive in low-tech setups. It propagates by fragmentation and provides excellent shelter for shrimp and fry. Regular trimming helps maintain its compact, flowing appearance, making it a favorite for aquascapers looking to add depth and movement to their designs.

Plant Info
Type Moss
Origin China
Growth Rate Slow to Moderate
Height 3 - 10 cm (1 - 4 inches), grows in a downward, cascading pattern
Light Demand Low to Medium
CO2 Low to Medium (5-20 mg/L), beneficial for denser growth
