pothos


Pothos (Epipremnum aureum) is a versatile and hardy aquatic plant known for its trailing vines and
heart-shaped, glossy leaves. While it is typically grown as a houseplant, Pothos thrives in aquariums
when its roots are submerged in water, with its vines extending above the surface. It is an excellent
choice for beginners due to its adaptability to a variety of conditions, from low to moderate lighting.
Pothos grows quickly and can reach several feet in length, making it ideal for creating a lush,
cascading effect in aquariums. It is also effective at purifying the water by absorbing excess
nutrients and chemicals, which helps maintain a healthier tank environment. While it doesn’t require
a substrate, adding some gravel or decorative elements can support its growth. Easy to maintain and
visually appealing, Pothos is a popular choice for aquascaping, adding both beauty and functionality
to any aquatic setup.

Plant Info
Type Vine Plant (can be grown submerged or emersed)
Origin Solomon Islands, Southeast Asia
Growth Rate Fast
Height Vines can grow up to several meters (in ideal conditions)
Light Demand Low to Medium
CO2 Low (does well in low-tech aquariums, but grows faster with CO₂)
