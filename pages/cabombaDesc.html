Cabomba


Cabomba is a fast-growing, feathery aquatic plant known for its delicate, fan-shaped green or reddish leaves. It can grow up to 50 cm tall, making it an excellent background plant for freshwater aquariums. Native to North and South America, Cabomba thrives in moderate to high lighting and benefits from CO₂ supplementation for optimal growth. It spreads by stem cuttings and requires regular trimming to maintain a bushy appearance. While it provides excellent shelter for fish and fry, its soft leaves can be fragile and may break easily if handled roughly. Cabomba adds a lush, flowing texture to aquascapes but requires stable water conditions to thrive.

Plant Info
Type Stem Plant
Origin North and South America
Growth Rate Fast
Height 30 - 80 cm (12 - 32 inches)
Light Demand Medium to High
CO2 Medium to High (15-30 mg/L) for optimal growth
