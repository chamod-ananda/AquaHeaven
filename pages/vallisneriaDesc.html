Vallisneria


Vallisneria, commonly known as "Val", is a fast-growing, grass-like aquatic plant that adds height and movement
to freshwater aquariums. With long, ribbon-like leaves that can reach up to 50-100 cm, it is best suited for background
placement. Vallisneria thrives in low to high lighting and adapts well to various water conditions, making it a great
choice for beginners. It spreads quickly through runners, forming dense underwater meadows. This plant prefers a
nutrient-rich substrate but does not require CO₂ supplementation. Its flowing leaves provide excellent cover for
fish, and gentle water currents enhance its natural swaying effect, creating a dynamic aquascape.

Plant Info
Type Rosette Plant
Origin Asia, Africa, North America
Growth Rate Fast
Height 20 - 100 cm (8 - 40 inches)
Light Demand Low to Medium
CO2 Low to Medium (5-15 mg/L), not required but beneficial
