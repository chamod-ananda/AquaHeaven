Glossostigma


Glossostigma elatinoides is a popular and highly regarded carpeting plant known for its tiny, bright green
leaves and dense growth habit, making it an ideal choice for creating lush, green foregrounds in aquariums.
This low-growing plant spreads quickly through runners, forming a thick, carpet-like mat that can reach
heights of just 2-5 cm, adding a natural, meadow-like appearance to the aquascape. Glossostigma thrives
in high lighting and benefits from CO₂ supplementation to encourage its vibrant color and compact growth.
It requires a nutrient-rich substrate and consistent maintenance, including trimming, to keep it healthy
and prevent overgrowth. Though it can be challenging to grow in low-tech setups, with the right conditions,
it can create a visually stunning, dense carpet. Its delicate appearance and fast-growing nature make
it a favorite for aquascapers seeking to achieve a professional, lush foreground in their planted tanks.

Plant Info
Type Carpet Plant
Origin Australia
Growth Rate Fast
Height 1 - 3 cm (0.4 - 1.2 inches)
Light Demand High
CO2 High (benefits greatly from CO₂ injection)
