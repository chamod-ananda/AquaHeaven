Duckweed


Lemna minor, commonly known as Duckweed, is a tiny, free-floating aquatic plant that rapidly multiplies,
forming a dense green carpet on the water's surface. Each plant consists of small, oval-shaped leaves (fronds)
about 1-5 mm in size, with delicate root filaments hanging below. Duckweed thrives in low to high lighting and
can absorb excess nutrients, helping to control algae growth. However, its fast-spreading nature requires
regular thinning to prevent it from blocking light for submerged plants. It serves as a natural food source
for fish and provides cover for small aquatic creatures. While beneficial for water quality, its rapid growth
can make it difficult to manage in some aquariums.

Plant Info
Type Floating Plant
Origin Worldwide (Cosmopolitan)
Growth Rate Very Fast
Height 1 - 5 mm (Tiny, floats on surface)
Light Demand Low to High
CO2 Low (absorbs CO₂ from air, does not require injection)
