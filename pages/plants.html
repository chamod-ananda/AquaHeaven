Varities of Aquarium Plants

  • Foreground Plants (Small, Carpert-Forming)

  • Midground Plants (Medium-sized, Bushy)

  • Background Plants (Tall, Vertical Growth)

  • Floating Plants (Free Floating, Surface Coverage)

  • Mosses (Attach to Rocks and Driftwood)

  • Stem Plants (Fast growing, Great for Oxygenation)

  • Rhizome Plants (Attach to Rocks and Driftwood)

  • Epiphytic Plants (Grow without Substrate)

  • Rosette Plants (Grow in a Circular Pattern from the Center)

  • Bulb & Tuber Plants (Grow from a Bulb, Unique Leaf Shapes)

  • Red & Colourful Plants (For Vibrant Aquascapes)

  • Submerged Plants (Fully Underwater, High Oxygenation)

  • Emersed & Semi-Aquatic Plants (Can Grow Above or Below Water)

  • Low-Light Plants (Easy Plants for Beginners)

  • High-Tech Plants (Require CO₂ & Strong Lighting)
