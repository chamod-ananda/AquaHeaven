Bucephalandra


Bucephalandra, commonly known as Buce, is a slow-growing, rhizome-based aquatic plant native to Borneo.
It features thick, wavy-edged leaves that range in color from deep green to shades of red, purple, or blue,
often with an iridescent sheen. Bucephalandra thrives when attached to rocks or driftwood, as burying its
rhizome in the substrate can cause it to rot. It prefers low to moderate lighting and benefits from CO₂
supplementation, though it can also survive in low-tech setups. Bucephalandra propagates by rhizome division
and grows best in stable water conditions with good circulation. Its compact size and striking colors make
it a popular choice for aquascaping, especially in nano tanks and natural-style layouts.

Plant Info
Type Rhizome Plant
Origin Borneo, Indonesia
Growth Rate Slow
Height 3 - 10 cm (1 - 4 inches)
Light Demand Low to Medium
CO2 Low (benefits from CO₂ injection for better growth)
