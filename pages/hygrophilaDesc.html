Hygrophila


Hygrophila, a hardy and fast-growing genus of stem plants, is a favorite among aquarists for its adaptability and lush foliage. Species like Hygrophila polysperma and Hygrophila corymbosa feature broad, lance-shaped leaves that range in color from bright green to reddish-brown under strong lighting. Thriving in moderate to high light, Hygrophila benefits from CO₂ supplementation but can also flourish in low-tech setups. Its rapid growth helps prevent algae by outcompeting it for nutrients. Easily propagated through stem cuttings, Hygrophila is ideal for midground or background placement, providing a natural, dense habitat for fish and shrimp while enhancing the overall aquascape.

Plant Info
Type Stem Plant
Origin Southeast Asia
Growth Rate Fast
Height 20 - 60 cm (8 - 24 inches)
Light Demand Low to Medium
CO2 Low (grows well without injection)
