Flame Moss


Taxiphyllum sp. ‘Flame’, commonly known as Flame Moss, is a unique aquatic moss distinguished by its upward-growing, twisted fronds that resemble flickering flames. Unlike other mosses that spread horizontally, Flame Moss grows in dense, vertical clusters, making it ideal for creating striking aquascaping effects. It thrives when attached to rocks, driftwood, or mesh and requires moderate to high lighting for optimal growth. While CO₂ supplementation enhances its development, it can also grow in low-tech setups. This moss provides excellent hiding spots for shrimp and fry and helps absorb excess nutrients, reducing algae growth. Regular trimming maintains its compact, fiery appearance, making it a popular choice for creative aquascapes.

Plant Info
Type Moss
Origin Southeast Asia
Growth Rate Slow to Moderate
Height 5 - 15 cm (2 - 6 inches), grows in an upward flame-like pattern
Light Demand Low to Medium
CO2 Low to Medium (5-20 mg/L), beneficial for healthier growth
