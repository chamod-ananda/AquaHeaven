Water Lettuce


Pistia stratiotes, commonly known as Water Lettuce, is a floating aquatic plant with soft, rosette-shaped, velvety green leaves that resemble lettuce. It has long, feathery roots that dangle beneath the surface, providing shelter for fish and fry while helping to absorb excess nutrients, reducing algae growth. Water Lettuce thrives in moderate to high lighting and prefers calm waters. It propagates quickly by producing small daughter plants connected by runners. Due to its rapid growth, regular thinning is necessary to prevent it from covering the entire surface and blocking light for submerged plants. While excellent for outdoor ponds, it requires careful management in aquariums to avoid overcrowding.

Plant Info
Type Floating Plant
Origin Tropical and Subtropical Regions (Possibly Africa)
Growth Rate Fast
Height 5 - 25 cm (2 - 10 inches), spreads over surface
Light Demand Medium to High
CO2 Low (absorbs CO₂ from air, does not require injection)
