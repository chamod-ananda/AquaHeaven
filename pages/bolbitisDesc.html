Bolbitis Fern


Bolbitis heudelotii, commonly known as Bolbitis Fern or African Water Fern, is a striking, slow-growing aquatic plant with delicate, dark green, finely divided leaves. Native to Africa, it thrives when attached to rocks or driftwood, as burying its rhizome in the substrate can cause it to rot. Growing up to 15-40 cm tall, it makes an excellent midground or background plant in aquascaping. Bolbitis prefers moderate to high lighting and benefits from good water flow and CO₂ supplementation to enhance its growth. Over time, it propagates through rhizome division, creating lush, flowing greenery that adds a natural, elegant touch to the aquarium.

Plant Info
Type Rhizome Plant
Origin Africa
Growth Rate Slow
Height 15 - 40 cm (6 - 16 inches)
Light Demand Low to Medium
CO2 Low to Medium (5-15 mg/L), beneficial for better growth
