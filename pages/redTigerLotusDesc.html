Red Tiger Lotus


Red Tiger Lotus (Nymphaea zenkeri) is a striking bulb plant admired for its broad, wavy-edged leaves
that range from deep red to vibrant purple, adding a bold focal point to aquariums. Growing up to
30-50 cm in height, it can be used as a midground or background plant, with its floating leaves creating
a natural, dappled lighting effect. Thriving in moderate to high lighting with nutrient-rich substrates,
Red Tiger Lotus benefits from CO₂ supplementation but can grow well in low-tech setups. It propagates
through bulb division, and regular pruning helps maintain its shape and prevent excessive floating leaf
growth. Its dramatic coloration and elegant leaf structure make it a favorite for aquascapers looking
to add contrast and depth to their planted tanks.

Plant Info
Type Bulb/Aquatic Plant
Origin Africa
Growth Rate Fast
Height 20 - 50 cm (8 - 20 inches)
Light Demand Medium to High
CO2 Medium (benefits from CO₂ injection for vibrant growth)
