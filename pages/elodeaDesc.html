Elodea


Elodea (Elodea canadensis) is a fast-growing, hardy aquatic plant commonly used in aquariums for its lush, green appearance and ability to oxygenate the water. With its slender, lance-shaped leaves arranged in whorls along its stems, Elodea creates a dense and natural look in aquariums, often serving as a background or midground plant. Growing rapidly in moderate to high lighting, it can reach up to 40 cm in height, but it can be trimmed to maintain a more compact size. Elodea thrives in nutrient-rich substrates but is also adaptable to a variety of water conditions, making it suitable for both low-tech and high-tech setups. It helps improve water quality by absorbing excess nutrients and provides shelter for small fish and invertebrates. Its low-maintenance nature and fast growth make it an excellent choice for those seeking to add a lively, green backdrop to their planted tanks.

Plant Info
Type Stem Plant
Origin North America, Europe
Growth Rate Fast
Height 20 - 60 cm (8 - 24 inches)
Light Demand Medium to High
CO2 Low to Medium (grows well without CO₂ injection)
