Java Fern


Microsorum pteropus, commonly known as Java Fern, is a hardy and low-maintenance aquatic plant popular among aquarists.
It features long, lance-shaped green leaves that grow from a thick rhizome, reaching heights of 20-35 cm. Unlike rooted
plants, Java Fern should be attached to driftwood or rocks, as burying its rhizome in the substrate can cause it to rot.
It thrives in low to moderate lighting and does not require CO₂ supplementation, making it ideal for beginners.
This slow-growing plant propagates through rhizome division and small plantlets that develop on mature leaves.
Its tough, leathery foliage is unappealing to herbivorous fish, making it a great choice for planted aquariums.

Plant Info
Type Rhizome Plant
Origin Southeast Asia
Growth Rate Slow
Height 20 - 35 cm (8 - 14 inches)
Light Demand Low to Medium
CO2 Low (5-15 mg/L), not required but beneficial
