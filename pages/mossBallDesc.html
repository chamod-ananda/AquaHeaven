Marimo Moss Ball


Marimo Moss Ball (Aegagropila linnaei) is a unique and charming aquatic plant that forms spherical,
moss-like balls, which float and roll gently in the water. Known for its soft, velvety texture and bright
green color, Marimo Moss Balls are a low-maintenance addition to any aquarium, thriving in low to moderate
lighting. They grow slowly, typically reaching 5-10 cm in diameter, and need regular gentle rolling or
turning to maintain their round shape and encourage healthy growth. Marimos are not rooted in the substrate
but instead float freely or rest on the aquarium floor. They help improve water quality by absorbing excess
nutrients and organic waste, making them an effective natural filter. Easy to care for and visually striking,
Marimo Moss Balls add a calming, natural touch to aquascapes while contributing to the overall health
of the tank.

Plant Info
Type Algae (Ball Form)
Origin Japan, Iceland, parts of Europe
Growth Rate Slow
Height 5 - 10 cm (2 - 4 inches) in diameter
Light Demand Low to Medium
CO2 Low (no need for CO₂ supplementation)
