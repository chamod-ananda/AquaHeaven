Amazon Sword


Echinodorus amazonicus, commonly known as the Amazon Sword, is a robust and popular aquatic plant known for its large, sword-shaped green leaves. It can grow up to 50 cm tall, making it ideal for midground or background placement in larger aquariums. Amazon Sword thrives in nutrient-rich substrates, as it absorbs nutrients primarily through its roots. It prefers moderate to high lighting and benefits from CO₂ supplementation for optimal growth, though it can also adapt to low-tech setups. This plant propagates by producing small plantlets on runners, which can be separated and replanted. With its broad leaves, it provides shelter for fish and enhances the natural aesthetic of any aquascape.

Plant Info
Type Rosette Plant
Origin South America
Growth Rate Moderate to Fast
Height 20 - 50 cm (8 - 20 inches)
Light Demand Medium
CO2 Low to Medium (10-20 mg/L), beneficial for growth
