Rotala


Rotala, a diverse genus of fast-growing stem plants, is prized in aquascaping for its vibrant colors and delicate, narrow leaves. Species like Rotala rotundifolia and Rotala indica display a range of hues from bright green to deep red, depending on lighting and nutrient levels. Under high lighting and CO₂ supplementation, Rotala develops compact, bushy growth with intense coloration, making it a striking midground or background plant. It propagates easily through stem cuttings, allowing for lush, dense arrangements in planted tanks. Rotala's graceful, flowing stems create a natural, dynamic appearance, enhancing the aesthetic appeal of freshwater aquariums.

Plant Info
Type Stem Plant
Origin Southeast Asia
Growth Rate Fast
Height 20 - 60 cm (8 - 24 inches)
Light Demand Medium to High
CO2 Medium (benefits from CO₂ injection for vibrant color)
