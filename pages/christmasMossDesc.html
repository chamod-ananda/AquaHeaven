Chistmas Moss


Vesicularia montagnei, commonly known as Christmas Moss, is a beautiful, slow-growing aquatic moss named for its branch structure, which resembles the shape of a Christmas tree. It forms dense, deep green, triangular fronds that attach to rocks, driftwood, or other surfaces, creating a lush, natural aquascape. Christmas Moss thrives in moderate to high lighting and benefits from CO₂ supplementation, though it can also grow in low-tech setups. It propagates by fragmenting, allowing it to spread gradually over time. This moss is highly popular in aquascaping for creating moss walls, carpets, or draping effects, and it provides excellent hiding spots for shrimp and fry. Regular trimming helps maintain its compact, bushy appearance.

Plant Info
Type Moss
Origin Southeast Asia
Growth Rate Slow to Moderate
Height 3 - 10 cm (1 - 4 inches), spreads horizontally
Light Demand Low to Medium
CO2 Low to Medium (5-20 mg/L), beneficial for denser growth
