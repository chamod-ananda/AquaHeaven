Banana Plant


The Banana Plant (Nymphoides aquatica) is a unique and eye-catching aquatic plant known for its
distinctive, rounded, banana-shaped rhizomes that grow just above the substrate. Its large,
heart-shaped floating leaves, which can reach up to 30 cm in diameter, create an attractive,
tropical vibe in aquariums. Banana plants thrive in moderate to high lighting and nutrient-rich
substrates, and they benefit from CO₂ supplementation for optimal growth. Although they are generally
low-maintenance, regular pruning helps maintain their shape and prevent excessive spread. The plant's
ability to produce small, yellow, fragrant flowers further enhances its charm. Ideal for the
foreground or midground, the Banana Plant adds both beauty and a sense of tranquility to any
aquascape.

Plant Info
Type Floating/Rhizome Plant
Origin Southeast Asia, India
Growth Rate Moderate
Height 15 - 30 cm (6 - 12 inches)
Light Demand Medium to High
CO2 Medium (benefits from CO₂ injection for better growth)
