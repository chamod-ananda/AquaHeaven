Pygmy Chain Sword


Pygmy Chain Sword (Helanthium tenellum) is a hardy, grass-like carpeting plant known for its delicate, narrow green leaves that create a lush, natural foreground in aquariums. Growing to a height of 5-10 cm, it spreads quickly through runners, forming a dense carpet that enhances the aesthetics of planted tanks. Thriving in moderate to high lighting with nutrient-rich substrates, it benefits from CO₂ supplementation but can adapt to low-tech setups. Regular trimming encourages even growth and prevents overcrowding. Its fast-spreading nature and vibrant green color make it a popular choice for aquascapers seeking a natural, meadow-like effect in their aquariums.

Plant Info
Type Rosette Plant (Carpeting)
Origin North & South America
Growth Rate Moderate to Fast
Height 3 - 10 cm (1 - 4 inches)
Light Demand Medium to High
CO2 Medium (benefits from CO₂ injection for better growth)
