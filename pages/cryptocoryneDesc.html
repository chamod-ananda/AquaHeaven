Cryptocoryne


Cryptocoryne, commonly called "Crypts," is a diverse genus of hardy, slow-growing aquatic plants known for
their wavy, lance-shaped leaves and adaptability to various conditions. Depending on the species, Crypts
can range from 5 cm to over 50 cm in height, making them suitable for foreground, midground, or background
placement in an aquarium. They thrive in low to moderate lighting and do not require CO₂ supplementation,
making them ideal for low-tech setups. Crypts prefer a nutrient-rich substrate and may experience "Crypt melt"
when introduced to a new tank, where leaves die back before regrowing stronger. With time, they form dense
clusters, enhancing the natural look of an aquascape.

Plant Info
Type Rosette Plant
Origin Asia (India, Sri Lanka, Indonesia, etc.)
Growth Rate Slow to Moderate
Height 5 - 40 cm (2 - 16 inches) depending on species
Light Demand Low to Medium
CO2 Low to Medium (5-15 mg/L), optional but improves growth
