Ludwijia


Ludwigia is a vibrant, fast-growing stem plant known for its striking red, orange, or green leaves, depending on the species and lighting conditions. It typically grows between 20-50 cm tall, making it ideal for midground or background placement in an aquarium. Ludwigia thrives in moderate to high lighting, with stronger light enhancing its red coloration. While CO₂ supplementation is not mandatory, it significantly boosts growth and color intensity. This plant propagates easily through stem cuttings, allowing aquarists to create dense, bushy clusters. Regular trimming encourages compact growth, and its bright hues add a stunning contrast to planted tanks.

Plant Info
Type Stem Plant
Origin North and South America
Growth Rate Fast
Height 20 - 50 cm (8 - 20 inches)
Light Demand Medium to High
CO2 Medium to High (15-30 mg/L) for best coloration and growth
