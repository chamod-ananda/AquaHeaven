Dwarf Baby Tears


Hemianthus callitrichoides (commonly known as Dwarf Baby Tears) is one of the smallest and most delicate carpeting
plants for freshwater aquariums. With tiny, round, bright green leaves, it forms a dense, cushion-like carpet across
the substrate, creating a vibrant and natural look. Native to Cuba, this plant spreads through creeping stems and requires
high lighting, CO₂ supplementation, and nutrient-rich substrates to thrive. It grows best when planted in small clusters
and can also be attached to rocks or driftwood for unique aquascaping effects. Regular trimming helps maintain its
compact growth and prevents it from lifting off the substrate.

Plant Info
Type Carpet Plant
Origin Cuba
Growth Rate Slow to Moderate
Height 3 - 5 cm (1 - 2 inches)
Light Demand High
CO2 High (25-30 mg/L) recommended for healthy growth
