Hornwort


Ceratophyllum demersum, commonly known as Hornwort, is a fast-growing, hardy aquatic plant that can be used as a floating plant or anchored in the substrate. It features long, thin, dark green stems covered in fine, needle-like leaves, giving it a feathery appearance. Hornwort is excellent for absorbing excess nutrients, helping to control algae growth, and providing shelter for fish and fry. It thrives in low to high lighting and does not require CO₂ supplementation, making it ideal for beginners. Due to its rapid growth, regular trimming may be needed to prevent overgrowth. Hornwort is also known to release allelopathic chemicals that can inhibit algae growth, making it a beneficial addition to planted tanks.

Plant Info
Type Stem/Floating Plant
Origin Cosmopolitan (Found worldwide)
Growth Rate Fast
Height 20 - 100 cm (8 - 40 inches), can grow indefinitely
Light Demand Low to High
CO2 Low (absorbs CO₂ from water, does not require injection)
