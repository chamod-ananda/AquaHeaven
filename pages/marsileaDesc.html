Marsilea


Marsilea is a versatile aquatic plant known for its unique, clover-like leaves. With several species commonly used
in aquariums, it can grow as a carpet or as an attractive background plant, depending on the type and conditions.
Marsilea typically features small, green, four-leaf clover shapes that can grow 5-10 cm tall and spreads via runners.
It thrives in low to moderate lighting, making it ideal for tanks with low-tech setups. With regular trimming,
it can create a dense ground cover. When planted in aquariums, Marsilea can adapt to both submerged and emersed growth,
providing a dynamic look to the aquascape. It prefers nutrient-rich substrates and may benefit from CO₂ supplementation
to encourage faster growth.

Plant Info
Type Carpet Plant
Origin Australia, Asia, and Africa
Growth Rate Moderate
Height 5 - 10 cm (2 - 4 inches)
Light Demand Medium to High
CO2 Medium to High (15-25 mg/L)
