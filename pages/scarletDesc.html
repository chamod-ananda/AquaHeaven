Scarlet Temple


Scarlet Temple (Alternanthera cardinalis) is a striking, red-stemmed plant that is highly sought after for its vibrant hues and dramatic appearance in aquariums. Its long, lance-shaped leaves range from green to brilliant red or pink, with color intensity increasing under high lighting and CO₂ supplementation. Growing up to 30-50 cm in height, Scarlet Temple makes an excellent choice for the midground or background, adding rich color and contrast to the aquascape. It thrives in nutrient-rich substrates and benefits from regular pruning to maintain its compact shape and encourage bushier growth. This plant prefers moderate to high lighting conditions, though it can adapt to lower light, with the color fading slightly. Its vibrant coloration and elegant form make it a popular choice for aquascapers seeking to create a bold focal point in their planted tanks.

Plant Info
Type Stem Plant
Origin Southeast Asia, India
Growth Rate Fast
Height 20 - 50 cm (8 - 20 inches)
Light Demand High
CO2 Medium to High (CO₂ injection enhances growth and color)
