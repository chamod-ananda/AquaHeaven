Rotala Wallichi


Rotala wallichii is a beautiful and vibrant stem plant known for its delicate, narrow leaves that transition from green to a brilliant pink or red hue when provided with high lighting and CO₂ supplementation. Growing up to 20-40 cm in height, it makes a stunning addition to the midground or background of an aquarium, where its fine, bushy appearance creates a soft, flowing effect. This plant thrives in nutrient-rich substrates and benefits from regular trimming to maintain its compact growth and encourage bushiness. Rotala wallichii prefers moderate to high lighting, and its red coloration becomes more pronounced under ideal conditions. While it can adapt to lower light, its color may fade. It is a favorite among aquascapers for its striking appearance and fast growth, making it an excellent choice for creating a dynamic, colorful contrast in planted tanks.

Plant Info
Type Stem Plant
Origin Southeast Asia
Growth Rate Moderate to Fast
Height 15 - 30 cm (6 - 12 inches)
Light Demand High
CO2 High (CO₂ injection is beneficial for vibrant growth)
