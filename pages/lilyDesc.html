Peace Lily


The Peace Lily (Spathiphyllum spp.) is a popular and elegant aquatic plant known for its glossy,
dark green leaves and distinctive white flowers that resemble a hooded spathe. Though typically
grown as a terrestrial plant, the Peace Lily can thrive in aquariums when its roots are submerged
in water, while the leaves remain above the surface. It grows up to 30-45 cm in height, making it
suitable for the midground or as a focal point in the aquarium. Peace Lilies prefer moderate lighting,
as too much light can cause the leaves to scorch, while too little can inhibit flowering. They benefit
from regular water changes and nutrient-rich substrates to support their growth. The plant is not
only visually striking but also helps purify the water by absorbing excess nutrients. Its elegant
flowers, paired with easy care, make the Peace Lily a favorite for aquascapers looking to add
beauty and functionality to their planted tanks.

Plant Info
Type Rhizome Plant (can be grown above or below water)
Origin Tropical Americas, Southeast Asia
Growth Rate Moderate
Height 30 - 60 cm (12 - 24 inches)
Light Demand Low to Medium
CO2 Low (does well in low-tech tanks, but benefits from some CO₂)
