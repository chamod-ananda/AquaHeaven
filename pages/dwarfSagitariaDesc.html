Dwarf Sagitaria


Dwarf Sagittaria (Sagittaria subulata) is a hardy, fast-spreading rosette plant known for its grass-like, bright green leaves that reach heights of 5-15 cm. Popular as a foreground or midground plant, it forms dense, carpet-like growth under moderate to high lighting. While CO₂ supplementation enhances its growth rate and leaf density, it can thrive in low-tech setups with nutrient-rich substrates. Dwarf Sagittaria propagates through runners, quickly covering the aquarium floor and creating a natural underwater meadow. Its adaptability and ease of care make it an excellent choice for both beginners and experienced aquascapers, adding lush greenery and depth to planted tanks.

Plant Info
Type Rosette Plant (Carpeting)
Origin North & South America
Growth Rate Moderate to Fast
Height 5 - 15 cm (2 - 6 inches)
Light Demand Low to Medium
CO2 Low (benefits from CO₂ injection for faster spreading)
