Frogbit


Frogbit is a floating aquatic plant with small, round, lily pad-like leaves that rest on the water’s surface.
Its smooth, slightly waxy leaves repel water, preventing them from becoming waterlogged. Beneath the surface,
long, feathery roots dangle, providing excellent shelter for fish and fry while absorbing excess nutrients
to help control algae. Frogbit thrives in moderate to high lighting and prefers still or slow-moving water.
It spreads quickly through runners, forming dense mats that require regular thinning to prevent light
blockage for submerged plants. Unlike Duckweed, Frogbit is easy to manage, making it a popular choice
for aquariums and ponds.

Plant Info
Type Floating Plant
Origin South America
Growth Rate Moderate to Fast
Height 5 - 10 cm (2 - 4 inches), spreads over surface
Light Demand Medium to High
CO2 Low to Medium (5-15 mg/L), beneficial for growth
