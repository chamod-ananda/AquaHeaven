Anubias Nana Pettie


Anubias nana ‘Petite’ is a miniature variant of Anubias barteri, prized for its compact size and hardy nature. With small, dark green, oval-shaped leaves growing on creeping rhizomes, it reaches a height of only 3-5 cm, making it perfect for nano aquariums and foreground placement. Unlike carpeting plants, it grows slowly and thrives when attached to rocks or driftwood rather than being buried in the substrate. This plant requires low to moderate lighting and does not need CO₂ supplementation, making it ideal for beginners. Its durable leaves resist herbivorous fish, and it adds a lush, natural touch to any aquascape.

Plant Info
Type Rhizome Plant
Origin West Africa
Growth Rate Slow
Height 3 - 5 cm (1 - 2 inches)
Light Demand Low to Medium
CO2 Low to Medium (5-15 mg/L)
