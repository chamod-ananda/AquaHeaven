Java Moss


Taxiphyllum barbieri, commonly known as Java Moss, is a popular, hardy aquatic plant known for its ability to thrive in a wide range of water conditions. Its dense, bright green growth consists of tiny, thread-like branches that form a lush carpet or attachment to rocks, driftwood, and other surfaces. Java Moss grows slowly but can spread over time, providing excellent hiding spots for fish, shrimp, and fry. It thrives in low to moderate lighting and does not require CO₂ supplementation, making it ideal for low-tech setups. Java Moss is also highly adaptable to different water parameters and can tolerate a variety of temperatures. It’s often used in aquascaping to create a natural look and help reduce algae growth by absorbing excess nutrients.

Plant Info
Type Moss
Origin Southeast Asia
Growth Rate Moderate to Fast
Height 5 - 10 cm (2 - 4 inches), can spread indefinitely
Light Demand Low to Medium
CO2 Low (5-15 mg/L), not required but beneficial
