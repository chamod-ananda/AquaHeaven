Dwarf Hair Grass



Eleocharis parvula, commonly known as Dwarf Hair Grass, is a carpeting aquatic plant that forms dense, grass-like mats in freshwater aquariums. With thin, bright green leaves reaching 3-10 cm in height, it creates a lush, natural foreground. Found in shallow waters and wetlands, it spreads through runners, making it an excellent choice for aquascaping. In an aquarium, regular trimming encourages compact growth and prevents over-shading. Dwarf Hair Grass thrives in nutrient-rich substrates with moderate to high lighting and CO₂ supplementation. It can be planted in small clumps for faster coverage, eventually forming a soft, lawn-like carpet that enhances the tank’s aesthetic.

Plant Info
Type Carpet Plant
Origin North America
Growth Rate Fast
Height 3 - 10 cm (1 - 4 inches)
Light Demand Medium to High
CO2 Medium to High (20-30 mg/L)
