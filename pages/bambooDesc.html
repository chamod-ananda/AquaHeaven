Lucky Bamboo


Lucky Bamboo (Dracaena sanderiana) is a resilient and decorative plant commonly used in aquariums and indoor spaces for its unique appearance and association with good fortune. Known for its long, slender stalks and vibrant green leaves, Lucky Bamboo thrives in water, where its roots can be submerged while the upper portion of the plant remains above the surface. It grows up to 30-45 cm in height and is ideal for smaller aquariums or as a centerpiece. Lucky Bamboo does best in low to moderate lighting, as direct sunlight can cause its leaves to scorch. It requires regular water changes to maintain healthy growth, as it is sensitive to chemicals like chlorine. While it doesn’t need a substrate, placing it in a decorative container or adding gravel can enhance its aesthetic appeal. Easy to care for and symbolizing good luck, this plant adds a touch of elegance and tranquility to any aquatic setup.

Plant Info
Type Rhizome Plant (often grown above water)
Origin Central Africa, Southeast Asia
Growth Rate Slow to Moderate
Height 30 - 90 cm (12 - 36 inches)
Light Demand Low to Medium
CO2 Low (does well in low-tech setups)
