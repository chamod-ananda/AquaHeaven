Anubias Barterii


Anubias barteri is a hardy, slow-growing aquatic plant known for its broad, dark green leaves and strong
adaptability. It typically grows 20-45 cm tall, making it a great choice for midground or background placement
in an aquarium. Unlike rooted plants, its thick rhizome should not be buried in the substrate; instead, it
thrives when attached to rocks or driftwood. Anubias barteri can tolerate low to moderate lighting and does
not require CO₂ supplementation, making it perfect for beginners. Its tough, leathery leaves resist herbivorous
fish, and it propagates by rhizome division, slowly spreading to create lush, natural aquascapes.

Plant Info
Type Rhizome Plant
Origin West Africa
Growth Rate Slow
Height 25 - 45 cm (10 - 18 inches)
Light Demand Low to Medium
CO2 Low (5-15 mg/L), not required but beneficial
