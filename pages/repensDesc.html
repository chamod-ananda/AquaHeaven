Ludwigia Repens


Ludwigia repens is a popular and versatile aquatic plant known for its attractive, red to green leaves that add a touch of color to any aquarium. This fast-growing stem plant thrives in a wide range of conditions, making it a great option for both beginners and experienced aquascapers. Its oval-shaped leaves, which grow in opposite pairs, create a lush and dense backdrop or midground effect, growing up to 30 cm in height. Ludwigia repens prefers moderate to high lighting and benefits from CO₂ supplementation to enhance its red coloration, though it can adapt to lower light with less intense color. It also thrives in nutrient-rich substrates and regular pruning helps maintain its compact growth. The plant’s ease of care, rapid growth, and striking color variation make it a popular choice for aquascaping, providing both beauty and structure to planted tanks.

Plant Info
Type Stem Plant
Origin North America, Central America
Growth Rate Moderate to Fast
Height 20 - 50 cm (8 - 20 inches)
Light Demand Medium to High
CO2 Medium to High (CO₂ injection improves growth and color)
